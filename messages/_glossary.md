## Notes for translators

- Medical questions (q1-q10) are safety-critical. Accuracy > style. When unsure, keep the English medical term with a local translation in parentheses.
- The waiver acknowledgment text has legal weight. Use formal register appropriate for legal documents in each locale.
- ICU syntax like `{count, plural, one {# diver} other {# divers}}` must be preserved exactly. Only translate the words inside the braces, not the syntax.
- Variables like `{name}`, `{operatorName}`, `{date}` are placeholders — keep them as-is.
