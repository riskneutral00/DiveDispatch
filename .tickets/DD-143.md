**Spec:**

`convex/portalDraft.ts:132` casts `profile.physicianClearanceRequired as boolean` on an `v.optional(v.boolean())` field. When `undefined`, the cast silently produces `undefined` (not `false`), which may confuse strict equality checks downstream.

Fix: Use `profile.physicianClearanceRequired ?? false`.

**Acceptance:**

- `physicianClearanceRequired` defaults to `false` when undefined
- Portal progress correctly reports no physician clearance needed for new profiles
